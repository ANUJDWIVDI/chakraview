2. B.Tech in Computer Science (AI & ML) – Key Details
Program Overview
Focuses on AI, Machine Learning, NLP, Robotics, and Data Analytics.


Prepares students for roles like AI Engineer, Data Scientist, Research Scientist.


Program Highlights
✅ Integrated Liberal Education (Psychology, Design Thinking)
✅ Hands-on projects (Drones, CANSAT Hackathon)
✅ Industry-aligned curriculum (NLP, Computer Vision, Deep Learning)
✅ Internships & Research (MoUs with top companies)


Eligibility
10+2 with PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Maths) + 45% marks (40% for SC/ST).


Career Paths
AI/ML Engineer


Data Scientist


Robotics Specialist


Business Intelligence Analyst
